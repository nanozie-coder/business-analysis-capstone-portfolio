# PayWave — Fintech Digital Transformation

> **Academic case study · Simulated organisation · 2026**

## Brief and business need

The assignment described a fintech with **500,000+ customers** offering digital wallets, P2P transfers, merchant payments, bill payments, savings and micro-lending. Reported business problems included transaction failures, customer complaints, delayed loan approvals, fraud incidents, regulatory compliance challenges and declining customer retention.

## Business Analysis scope

- Stakeholder analysis and elicitation strategy; **no actual stakeholder interviews were conducted**
- Proposed BRD with **18 business requirements**
- **52 functional**, **28 non-functional** requirements and requirements traceability
- Six journey models: payments, merchant settlement, lending, fraud/security, support, financial/regulatory assurance
- **34 user stories**, with acceptance criteria and test scenarios
- SWOT/TOWS, gap analysis, business capability assessment, Business Model Canvas
- Business case, risk register, implementation roadmap and solution evaluation plan

## Recommendation

**Option B: modular modernisation**, with controls for exactly-once financial posting, truthful payment finality, reconciled wallet and merchant balances, approved lending decisions, auditable fraud interventions and customer redress. Funding should proceed through discovery, design and a restricted pilot before broader rollout.
