# Business Analysis Capstone Portfolio

**Ndubuisi Anozie** | Business Analysis • Data Analytics • Operational Performance

This repository presents two **academic, simulated business-transformation case studies**. It showcases structured Business Analysis methods and decision artefacts rather than live client delivery.

## Case studies

| Project | Industry | Business problem | Selected output |
|---|---|---|---|
| [PayWave — Digital Financial Services](paywave-fintech/README.md) | Fintech | Transaction failures, lending delays, fraud, complaints and retention | 18 business requirements, 52 functional and 28 non-functional requirements, 34 user stories |
| [Smart Retail Connect — Retail Transformation](smart-retail-connect/README.md) | Retail | Inefficiencies, customer dissatisfaction, digital competition | 14 business requirements, 38 functional and 24 non-functional requirements, 24 user stories |

Both capstones produced **20 numbered deliverables** and an executive presentation. See [the three-page portfolio summary](portfolio/Business_Analysis_Capstone_Portfolio_Featured.pdf) for a recruiter-friendly overview.

## Methods and competencies

- Business problem definition; stakeholder mapping; communication and elicitation planning
- Requirements engineering, MoSCoW, user stories, Given/When/Then acceptance criteria
- As-Is/To-Be process mapping, gap and business capability analysis
- SWOT/TOWS and Business Model Canvas
- Options appraisal, cost-benefit analysis, NPV sensitivity and risk mitigation
- Implementation roadmaps, change management and benefits evaluation

## Evidence and integrity

All businesses in this repository are **case-study scenarios**. Internal operating data, maturity scores, stakeholder responses and investment outcomes were not observed in real companies unless explicitly described as sourced external industry context. Figures labelled *illustrative* are planning exercises, not verified financial results. This repository does not present case-study assignments as commercial consulting engagements.
