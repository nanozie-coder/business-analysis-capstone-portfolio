# Smart Retail Connect — Retail Digital Transformation

> **Academic case study · Simulated organisation · 2026**

## Brief and business need

The assignment described an **18-branch retailer in three unspecified states**, selling groceries, electronics, household items and fashion. It reported operational inefficiencies, declining customer satisfaction and growing digital competition.

## Business Analysis scope

- Stakeholder analysis, communications and elicitation planning; real interviews were **not** conducted
- Proposed BRD with **14 business requirements**
- **38 functional** and **24 non-functional** requirements with traceability
- Proposed As-Is / To-Be models for checkout, inventory/replenishment, complaints/returns and reporting
- **24 user stories** with acceptance criteria
- SWOT/TOWS, gap analysis, capability maturity, Business Model Canvas
- Options appraisal, risk management, implementation roadmap, KPI and benefits evaluation

## Recommendation

**Option B: phased configurable integrated retail platform**. Priorities include checkout accuracy, inventory visibility, controlled replenishment, service-case tracking, reliable management data and staff readiness. Conduct discovery and a limited representative-branch pilot before scaling.
